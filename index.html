About the conference

Come and join us for the Asia Awareness Conference, April 5–7, 2027, where you can gather with Asian pastors and leaders, field workers serving in Asia, and pastors, leaders, and church members from the U.S. church to See the nations, Love the people, and Reach the unreached together. Over three days at Living Water Foursquare Church in Olympia, Washington, we will open our eyes to what God is doing across South Asia, North Asia, Southeast Asia, and the South Pacific, let our hearts be moved with compassion for the people behind the numbers, and pray together in unity.

This is a chance to celebrate what God is doing now and to seek the Lord for what He wants to do next as we examine the needs and opportunities we have to be a part of bringing the gospel to unreached communities across Asia. Show up ready to learn what God is doing across the nations, let your heart be stirred with compassion for the unreached, and explore how you and your community can engage together to the unreached.

Why Asia

Where the need is greatest, and where the family is already sending

Across South Asia, North Asia, Southeast Asia, and the South Pacific, our four Asia regions encompass over 40 nations and 4.4 billion people, including roughly 4,300 of the world's 7,250 Unreached People Groups (UPGs), representing two-thirds of the entire unreached world. This conference is a chance to See these nations, Love the people, and Reach the unreached together.

We aren't starting from scratch, and we aren't going alone. As part of our shared mission with the global Foursquare church, workers are being sent from around the world throughout Asia. Leaders from these nations will be with us in Olympia and sharing what God is doing to expand His Kingdom. This is an invitation to learn from one another, pray together, and discover our unique roles in this global partnership.

Who it's for

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who should come?

Pastors, regional pastors, ministry leaders, church members, and anyone sensing a call, preparing to go, or already serving. You'll gather with Asian pastors and leaders, field workers serving in Asia, and the U.S. church to see, love, and reach the unreached together.

How much does it cost?

Full Conference pricing is still being finalized and includes both dinners. Evening Sessions Only is $20 with a meal when you register in advance, or $5 at the door for the session only. If cost is a barrier, please reach out.

Are meals included?

Full Conference registration includes Monday's welcome dinner and Tuesday's Indian dinner. Tuesday lunch is on your own during a two-hour window, with plenty of options nearby. Evening registration ($20) includes dinner.

Where should I stay?

See our list of nearby hotels. Host homes with Living Water families are also available on request. Just let us know when you register.

What should I bring?

A Bible, a notebook or device for notes, a water bottle and layers for Northwest spring weather. Bring a friend or a team from your church, too.

How can I get my church involved?

Bring a team, pray with us, and talk with FMI leaders at the conference about how you and your community can engage together to the unreached: through prayer, equipping, and sending workers. Email us and we'll help you take the next step.
